Managing your crew

Hire sailors at a port's Tavern. Every sailor has a role, a level, traits, health and morale. Assign them to stations (sails, guns, helm and so on) yourself, or press 1 to 5 to give an order (Sail, Battle, Repair, Board, Rest) and they pick stations themselves.

Upkeep

Once a day ({{day_minutes}} real minutes) you pay every sailor's wage, and each sailor eats one food and drinks one water. A galley cuts how much food is eaten. Missed wages, hunger and thirst hurt morale; hungry crew also lose health.

Morale

Paid wages, good food, grog, a galley with a cook, a figurehead and a shantyman keep morale up. Unhappy sailors work worse, and a sailor whose morale drops below 10 may desert when you next dock (loyal sailors never do).

Experience

Sailors gain experience by working their stations and get better as they level up.
