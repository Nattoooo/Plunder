The world

The ocean is 1,000 × 1,000 miles, generated from one fixed seed, so it is the same for everyone. Danger rises toward the edges of the map, and so do the rewards.

Seas

{{biomes}}
  • Emerald Shallows: warm, calm water in the middle of the map, where you start.
  • Coral Expanse: bright eastern shallows full of reefs.
  • Frostreach: cold northern water with glaciers and ice floes.
  • Ashen Reach: southern volcanic islands and black sand.
  • Mistveil: foggy western water haunted by ghost ships.

Factions and notoriety

Sinking merchant ships raises your notoriety by 10 and sinking navy ships by 15; sinking pirates lowers it a little. Notoriety falls by 1 every minute. At 60 or more the pirate Brethren treat you as one of their own and leave you alone.

Day and weather

A full day lasts {{day_minutes}} real minutes. Rain, storms and fog come and go. Fog is thickest in the Mistveil.
