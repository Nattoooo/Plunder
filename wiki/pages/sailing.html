Sailing

Press W to draw the sail one step (Furled, Half, Full) and S to furl it. Hold A or D to turn the sail; the ship turns with it and holds that heading until you turn again.

Wind

The wind changes direction and strength over time. Each sail type gets a different share of its power depending on where the wind comes from relative to your heading (0° means straight from behind):

{{sails}}

Square sails are fastest with the wind behind them. Lateen rigs keep more power across and into the wind. Oar benches move you regardless of wind, even with the sails furled: press Q to row.

Speed

Speed comes from your total sail power against your ship's mass and drag; longer hulls are faster. Heavy cargo, flooding and storms slow you down. Skilled crew on the sails and at the helm make you faster and turn sharper.

Hazards

  • Coral reefs in shallow water scrape and damage your hull.
  • Ice floes in the Frostreach block ships.
  • Running aground at speed damages the hull.
  • Storms push you off course, slow you down and can damage your ship.
  • The edge of the world is dark water you cannot cross.

Time and weather

A full day lasts {{day_minutes}} real minutes. Nights are dark; lanterns, fires and towns give light. Rain, storms with lightning, and fog come and go.
