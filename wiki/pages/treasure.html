Treasure

  • Flotsam: crates and barrels floating at sea. Sail over them to pick them up.
  • Messages in bottles: they often hold treasure maps.
  • Treasure maps: they mark an X on an island. Go ashore and dig there with a shovel.
  • Dig spots: sparkling mounds on islands. Dig them with a shovel too.
  • Chests: found on islands. Some are locked and need a lockpick set, which can snap. Some are guarded by creatures.
  • Shrines: hold relics.
  • Wrecks: salvage them at sea. A diver aboard finds much more.

Most of these come back after a while. See the loot tables for what each source can drop.
