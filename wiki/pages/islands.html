Owning islands

Many islands have a claim stone. Go ashore, walk up to the stone and press E to buy the island. Bigger islands cost more and have room for more buildings, from 2 up to 8.

Buildings keep producing in real time while you are away, until the island's storage is full. Every island stores 100 goods, plus 300 for each Warehouse. Visit the island to collect what was made.

  • A Private Dock lets you repair and resupply there, and you respawn there if it is closer than your last port.
  • A Coastal Fort fires on hostile ships near the island.
  • A Tavern lets you hire crew there.
