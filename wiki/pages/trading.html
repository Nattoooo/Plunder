Trading and contracts

Every port has a market. Goods are cheap where they are produced and dear elsewhere; each item's page lists the seas where it is cheapest. Prices react to your trades, so selling a lot of one good in one port lowers its price there.

Your cargo space comes from cargo holds. A quartermaster earns you more loot and lowers wages.

See the list of trade goods.

Contracts

Each port has a bounty board with three kinds of work:

  • Bounties: hunt down and sink a named captain.
  • Deliveries: carry goods to another port.
  • Salvage: recover a strongbox from a wreck.

Contracts pay gold and fame.
