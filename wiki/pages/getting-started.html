Tips

  • Once the sail is drawn, your ship keeps sailing on its own. Furl the sail with S to slow down, or drop anchor with X to stop.
  • Sail with the wind behind you whenever you can. See Sailing.
  • Your crew eats and drinks every day. Buy food and water before long trips. See Managing your crew.
  • Fame unlocks bigger keels and better parts. You earn it by sinking ships, finishing contracts, finding treasure and visiting new ports.
  • The game saves itself in your browser every so often and when you close the tab.
