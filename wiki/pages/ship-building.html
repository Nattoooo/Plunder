Ship building

At any port's Shipwright you can redesign your ship on a grid. First pick a keel, which sets how many deck cells you have to build on:

{{keels}}

Fill the cells with a hull material, then place parts on them. Every part has a price, needs some materials and may need fame.

Placement rules

  • Cannons must sit on the edge of the hull, facing out.
  • The rudder goes at the stern; the iron ram and figurehead go at the bow.
  • A ship can have only one helm and one figurehead.

What your design decides

  • Speed: sail power against mass and drag.
  • Turning: rudder, helm and lateen sails.
  • Cargo and crew space: cargo holds and crew quarters.
  • Firepower: guns and their firing arcs.

Parts with a crew station work best when someone is assigned there, and some work partly without anyone. See Managing your crew.

The Shipwright also repairs your ship for a fee.
