Combat

Hold the left mouse button to fire every gun that can aim at the cursor. Each gun has a firing arc and a reload time. The crosshair turns green when a loaded gun can fire, orange when guns bear but are reloading, and grey when nothing bears.

{{guns}}

Ammunition

Press R to swap the ammunition your cannons use. Swivel guns need no ammunition; mortars fire mortar shells.

{{ammo}}

Damage

Shots hit individual hull cells and parts. Broken parts stop working until repaired, parts can catch fire, and holes let in water. A ship sinks when its hull falls to {{sink_at}} of its maximum. A carpenter in a workshop repairs damage at sea; any Shipwright repairs everything for a fee.

Boarding

Press F beside a crippled, slow or surrendered ship to board it. Your crew's fighting strength decides the fight, and marines are the best fighters. Merchant ships often surrender when badly damaged.

Ramming

Sailing into another ship hurts both of you. An iron ram at the bow triples the damage you deal.

Forts

Port forts fire on ships that are hostile to the port. You can build your own Coastal Fort on an island you own.

On foot

On land, left-click to attack with your equipped weapon. If you are knocked out, your crew carries you back aboard and you lose 10% of your gold. See Creatures.

If your ship sinks

  • You lose 15% of your gold.
  • All trade goods, treasure, relics and materials in your hold sink with the ship. You can sail back to the spot to recover them.
  • Each crew member has a 30% chance to drown.
  • You return to your last port, or to your own island if it has a Private Dock and is closer.
