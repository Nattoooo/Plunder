# grass-tuft (4x2, flexible, unchanged)
- Row 0: two `h` blade tips at the outer corners, flicking outward like a tuft's tallest blades.
- Row 1: `G` outer blade bases with two `h` centre blades between them; symmetric.
- Only foliage swap colours (h, G), no outline since it is a ground mark and tiny; mid and dark greens keep it low contrast on grass.
