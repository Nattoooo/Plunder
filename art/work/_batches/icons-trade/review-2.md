# Review round 2: icons-trade

Only the six families that failed round 1 are re-reviewed; the other ten stay passed. Set notes: the detail gap is mostly closed. Sack material now matches (sugar and spices share F/D/d), whale-oil, furs and indigo have form. Silk is still the weak sprite of the set.

## spices: PASS
Round 1: sand ramp recoloured (fixed); k row 5 replaced by a 6 px f tie (fixed), the heap now flares from the tie like sugar's mouth.
MINOR: spices (2-3,3)-(4,4): the heap outline steps 2 px in one row (cols 2 to 4), slightly abrupt. A k at (3,4) would smooth it.

## silk: FAIL
Round 1: issue 1 (bricks, BLOCKER) not fixed in effect; issue 2 (double k line) fixed; issue 3 (diagonal noise) partly fixed; issue 4 (salmon colour) partly fixed.
1. [MAJOR] silk (0-13,1-6) and (2-15,7-12): the bolts still read as two red bars/bricks at 1x. The "end cap" is a 4x4 E block with a straight left edge (col 0 over rows 3-5 and col 2 over rows 9-11), and a full-height R line at col 5 (rows 2-5) and col 7 (rows 8-11) cuts it off like a book spine. There is no round cap and no visible top surface of the roll. Fix: draw the cap as a real ellipse: 5 px tall with 1 px stepped corners, cap fill E with a single R spiral arc (e.g. 2 px at (2,4),(3,4) plus (3,3)), then remove the straight R column at col 5/7 and let the body start directly against the cap's right outline k at col 5/7; the body keeps E top row, e middle, R bottom row.
2. [MAJOR] silk (1-5,2-5) and (3-7,8-11): the cap pattern "ERREE" / "EEEER" is a scatter of 1-2 px R fragments with no coherent spiral (R at (2,3),(2,4)? plus (5,*) stripe). It reads as noise rather than wound cloth. Fix: one clean spiral, a single 2-3 px R arc inside an E ring, nothing else.
3. [MINOR] silk (0-1,7) and (14-15,7): the shared k row 7 runs 1 px beyond both bolts at the ends (cols 1-2 and 14-15 act as lone stubs). Trim when the bolts are rounded.
4. [MINOR] The two bolts are identical copies offset by 2 px and aligned top to bottom; a 1 px shift of the cap fill on the second bolt would help the pair look hand-stacked.
