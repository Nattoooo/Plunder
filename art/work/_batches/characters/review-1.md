# Batch `characters`, review round 1

Read: STYLE.md, CRITIC.md, brief.md, approved.png, sheet-1.png (zoomed 2x for the captain), all six grids in art/src and their notes, captain critique-3, house-cottage notes/grid. All sizes in this batch are flexible, so the size drift is not flagged by itself. Where the drift causes a scale problem it is raised as one.

Set-level notes: the outline is a consistent 1 px `k` and the palette characters are legal everywhere. Light is top-left throughout. Detail level is NOT consistent: captain and crab are rendered with shading, while skeleton, wraith and serpent are close to flat slabs (large single-tone areas, 3 px silhouettes on a 7-9 px canvas). The approved set (tree, cottage, boulder) reads as solid chunky shapes with clear silhouettes. Four of the six families here fail the silhouette test.

## captain: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] all 8 sprites, scale: the captain is 9 x 9 with a 7 px body, but the approved house-cottage door is 4 px wide (cols 8-11, rows 15-22) and STYLE.md says "a door fits the captain". The captain does not fit, and critique-3 issue 1 (the same MAJOR) is still unresolved: specs.json still says 6 x 9 and no door/scale decision is recorded. Fix: either redraw the captain at 6-7 px total width (body 5 px, hat wings no wider than the body, face interior 3 px), or record 9 x 9 in specs.json and widen the door in house-cottage and every door-bearing building to at least 7 px and re-review them. The decision must be made now; the captain is the scale unit for the whole land set.
2. [MAJOR] captain-down-1/2/3 (1..7,2): row 2 `kyyyOyyOk` is a solid 7 px gold bar, plus gold wings at (1,1) and (7,1). Gold is about 9 px against 3 px of black `K` crown, so at 1x the head reads as a gold-banded cap or a crown, not the brief's "black tricorn hat with gold trim". The back views (`kykkkkkOk`) already show the right black-hat-with-gold-points look, so front and back no longer look like the same hat. Fix: row 2 becomes `kykkOkkOk` (black brim, gold only at the two corners and the front point at (4,2)); keep eyes on row 3.
3. [MINOR] captain-up-2 (6,5) `S` and captain-up-3 (2,5) `s`: the swinging hand sits one row higher than in down-1/2/3 (row 6) and beside the queue, reading as a stray skin pixel on the back. Fix: move it to row 6 (6,6)/(2,6) as in the front views.
4. [MINOR] captain-down-2 (2,7)-(2,8) and captain-down-3 (6,7)-(6,8): the lifted leg is a single `k` pixel (the boot) with no trouser tone, so the stride reads as one leg thinning. Accepted in critique-3 as a step; keep, but check once the scale decision above is made.
5. [MINOR] captain-side-1 row 8 `..kkkkk..`: both feet are fused into a 5 px black bar, so standing in profile shows no legs. Fix: `..kk.kk..` is not possible with the 2 px trousers above; make (4,8) `.`: `..kk.kk..` and extend the trousers to `.kRRbbk..` unchanged.
6. [MINOR] hat wings (0,0)-(1,2) and (7,0)-(8,2) in the front and back views still read slightly as ears or horns at 1x. Accepted in critique-3; unchanged.

Frame consistency otherwise holds: down-3 mirrors down-2, up-2/3 mirror each other, heads and hats are identical per view. Palette clean.

## skeleton: FAIL
1. [BLOCKER] skeleton-1/2 silhouette: filled black it is a 9 x 7 rectangle with a head bump. The arms are 1 px bone columns at cols 1 and 7 separated from the 3 px torso (cols 3-5) by `k` columns, so rows 3-6 read as vertical stripes (barcode or striped egg on the sheet), not a skeleton. The ribcage is narrower than the skull (5 vs 7 px) and the shoulders do not connect to the arms. Fix: redraw the torso 5 px wide (cols 2-6) with bone ribs `x`/`K` bars across rows 4-6, and attach the arms at (1,3)/(7,3) touching the shoulder, hanging 1 px clear of the ribs only from row 4 down.
2. [MAJOR] skeleton-1/2 legs (rows 7-8): the legs are two 1 px `k` pixels with no bone tone, so there are no legs, only a pelvis and feet; the skeleton is skull plus 4 rows of ribs. Frame 2 (`...k.kk..`) differs from frame 1 (`..kk.k...`) by one outline pixel, so there is no readable stride. Fix: shorten the ribcage by one row and give the legs 3 rows of `x` bone with a `k` foot; frame 2 mirrors the stride.
3. [MAJOR] skeleton-1/2: the brief says "Skeleton pirate" and there is no pirate cue at all (notes say none added). Fix: add a tricorn or bandana on the skull (rows 0-1) in `k`/`R`, or a 1 px cutlass `N` in the right hand.
4. [MINOR] skull (3,2),(5,2) `x` teeth alternate with `k`: reads as a comb at 1x. Fix: row 2 `.kkxxxkk.`-style 3 px teeth.

## wraith: FAIL

Fix: round the hood (row 1 `.kiiik.`, row 2 full) and taper the robe so row 4-6 flare out, e.g. a 5 px hood over a 7 px skirt, with the sleeves or hem breaking the edge.
2. [MAJOR] wraith-1/2 (2..4,2..4): the face void is a T shape (`KKK` over single `K` pixels at (3,3),(3,4)) and the eyes `I` at (2,3),(4,3) sit on the teal robe, not inside a dark opening, so it reads as a smiling mask with a nose line, not a hood hiding eyes. Fix: make the opening a 3 x 3 `K` block (rows 2-4, cols 2-4) with `I` eyes at (2,3) and (4,3) inside it.

Fix: hem row 7 as `.kiki k.`-style tatters: three `i` pixels with `k` beneath, shifted one pixel between frames, and close the outline under row 6.
4. [MINOR] the robe is a single flat `i` slab with no shadow side; add `i` lit left and a darker tone only if the palette allowed one (teal ramp has none), so consider shading the right column with `K`-free pixels or leave.
