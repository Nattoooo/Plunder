# island-1 review, round 1

Set-level notes (apply to all five): no family is shippable as is. The sheet shows the huts (plantation, fishery, distillery) sharing the cottage's door and window, which is good, but wall heights and chimney/crop/fish details drift from the approved look (bold, few shapes, 1 px details that name something real). Several `notes.md` files claim fixes that are NOT in `art/src/*.txt` (plantation "D removed / y removed", lumber-camp and mine "W changed to v"). The sources are what ship; the notes are wrong. Sizes: plantation 37x21 (spec 37x22), lumber-camp 32x12, mine 30x13, fishery 31x21, distillery 26x23 are all within spec or flexible.

## plantation: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] plantation crops (16..21, 23..28, 30..35 by rows 0..19): each crop is four vertical stripes H|h|y/h|G running the whole height, parallel to the outline. That is banding, and it reads as a green pill or cactus, not sugar cane or corn. Fix: reduce each crop to two tones (H lit left column, G right column, h only as a 1 px step), and break the stalk with 1 px leaf notches (a `k` or `G` pixel sticking out at alternating sides on 2 or 3 rows) so it reads as a plant.
2. [MAJOR] plantation crops: `y` ear pixels are still repeated on about 8 rows per crop (e.g. x19 rows 4,7,10,11,13,16 of crop 1; same pattern in crops 2 and 3). This is exactly the repeated speckle STYLE.md forbids, and notes.md wrongly says it was removed. Fix: keep `y` only on 1 or 2 ears near the top of each crop (rows 4..6) and turn every other `y` into `h` or `G`.
3. [MAJOR] plantation (17..20, 19), (24..27, 19), (31..34, 19): the base row is still `DDDD` inside a `k` box, so each crop stands in a small outlined tub; notes.md says these were removed. Fix: replace the 12 `D` with `G`/`g` or make the soil one continuous `D` strip across all three crops, or drop the box outline so it reads as ground.
4. [MINOR] plantation crops: three identical crops differing only in height look stamped; also the 1 px tip spike at (25,0). Fix: stagger tip shapes (2 px tip on one crop, flat on another).

## lumber-camp: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] lumber-camp shed (14..15, 4..10): right post is `w` outer-left, `W` right, i.e. the lighter tone is on the right/shadow side. The left post is lit on the left (`V`,`w`), so the two posts are lit from opposite sides. Notes claim W was changed to v; it was not. Fix: make the right post `v` (14) and `w` (15) at rows 4..10 so shadow is on the right.
2. [MAJOR] lumber-camp bench log (5..13, 6): the log on the bench is a single row of `FFDDDDDD` squeezed between the `k` line at row 5 and the bench top `V` at row 7. It has no end ring, no round form and no outline, so it reads as a shelf stripe; combined with the solid `K` interior the shed reads as a closed market stall or chest, not an open shed with a log on a bench. Fix: remove the log or make it 2 rows tall (rows 5..6 `F`/`D` with a `k` underline at row 7), and open the interior by dropping the bench entirely so `K` is a clear dark gap between two posts.
3. [MINOR] lumber-camp log ends (19..31, 3..10): F/D "rings" are just F top-left / D elsewhere, not rings; acceptable at this size but the pile is the only readable part. Fix: add one `d`-free dark `D` pixel at the centre of each end if wanted; otherwise leave.

## mine: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] mine hillside (1..5 and 18..22, rows 3..11): rock tones run as vertical bands parallel to the dome outline (U U T T T on the left, T T t t t on the right). That is banding. Fix: use one light patch top-left (`U` in a 2..3 px blob near (2..4, 2..5)), `T` as the base everywhere else, and `t` only along the right edge and bottom.
2. [MAJOR] mine frame (15..16, 4..11): right post is `w` then `W`, lighter on the shadow side, opposite to the left post (`V`,`w`). Notes claim `W` to `v`; source still has `W`. Fix: make the right post `v` (15) and `w` (16) for rows 4..11.
3. [MAJOR] mine ore cart (22..29, 6..12): the cart box shares its outline with the hill (k at x22/23), so it is jammed into the rock; the wheels at (23..25, 11) and (27..29, 11) are 3 px `kMk` blobs fused with the bottom outline, and the second wheel is flush with the right edge while the first sits near the hill, so the cart is not symmetric on its wheels. The cart reads as a stool or anvil. Fix: leave 1 px transparent gap between hill and cart (shift cart right by 1 and widen the sprite to 31 if needed), put wheels at x24 and x28 as `M` with `k` below, one pixel inset from the box ends.
4. [MINOR] mine floor rail (10..13, 11): a 4 px `n` bar inside the opening reads as a step, not a rail. Fix: extend two `n` pixels out through the door row 12 or remove it.

## fishery: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] fishery fish (18..27, 7..10): the three "fish" are 2 px wide `LN`/`ln` stripes hanging straight from the beam. They have no head, eye, tail or taper and read as blue-white laundry or flags. Fish 1 has `k` on both sides (17 and 20) while fish 2 (22..24) and 3 (26..28) have only a right `k`, so they are not consistent. Fix: draw each fish as 3 wide x 4 tall: 1 px `k` string pixel at the top, `N` head, `L` body, `n` belly, tail `k` 2 px wide at the bottom, same shape x3 spaced 1 px apart, all outlined identically.
2. [MAJOR] fishery hut (0..13, 5..20): the hut wall is 15 rows tall for 12 px wide with a roof on top: it is a narrow tower beside the cottage-proportioned huts of plantation (wall 10 rows) and distillery (wall 10 rows). It looks stretched to fill the 21 px height; size is flexible so there is no reason. Fix: cut the wall to 10 rows (remove rows 6..10 of blank wall above the window, giving height 16) and let the rack set the height, or lower the rack posts to match.
3. [MINOR] fishery rack: beam (15..30, 5..6) is `V`/`v` outlined and the interior posts are `k` bordered, but the rack is 16 px of mostly empty space with no cross brace. Acceptable, but a 1 px diagonal brace `w` at (17..18, 11..12) would anchor it.

## distillery: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] distillery chimney (9..12, 0..10): the chimney rises 11 rows above the roof of an 8 px door hut (wall about 10 rows), about twice the hut roof height, and is split by an arbitrary `kkkk` band at (9..12, 3) and a flat `kkkk` cap at row 0. The notes show it was lengthened only to reach 23 px, although size is flexible. It reads as a pole, and the roof is cut off by it (the roof has no right slope). Fix: shorten the chimney to 5 rows above the roof (start at row 6), delete the mid band at row 3, give the roof a proper right edge and `k` cap, keep the sprite height at about 19 with the pot's base on the bottom row.
2. [MAJOR] distillery pot still (14..25, 6..19): the silhouette is a vase or amphora. A pot still needs a lyne arm leaving the top of the neck; as drawn the neck is closed flat at (17..22, 6) with `kkkkkk` and nothing leaves it. Fix: add a 2 px thick `O`/`o` pipe with `k` outline running from the neck top (22, 7) bending right 1..2 px and down, or give a conical lid with a 1 px `y` highlight, so it reads as a still.
3. [MINOR] distillery firebox (15..24, 20..22): the opening `K` at (17..20, 21) is left of centre (centre of sprite part is 19.5) and the flame is one `qQ` row; the right `TTT` is wider than the left `U`. Fix: centre the opening on x 18..21 and add a `Q` pixel above the flame at (19..20, 20).
