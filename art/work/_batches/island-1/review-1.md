# Review island-1, round 1

Batch-level: all five sizes match spec exactly (74x44, 64x23, 60x26, 62x42, 52x46). Outline, top-left light, ramps and level of detail are broadly coherent, and the three plaster huts (plantation, fishery, distillery) share one build. Two recurring failures sink the batch. (1) Clipped silhouettes: in every sprite the art runs into a canvas edge with no `k` outline. Sizes are flexible, so widen/heighten the canvas and close the outline; do not crop the art. (2) The huts put the door in the right-hand corner of the front wall, unlike the approved house-cottage (door centred, windows symmetric about it, 1 px knob, lit-left door ramp). Hut doors, windows and roofs should be reconciled once and copied to all three huts.

## plantation: FAIL
1. [BLOCKER] plantation stalk 1 top (34,0)-(35,0): the top row is `H`,`h` fill with no outline above it; the stalk is clipped by the canvas. Fix: add one row at the top (canvas 74x45, shift everything down 1) and put `kk` above (34,0)-(35,0) so the tip is `k` outlined; keep the bottom on the last row.
2. [MAJOR] plantation hut door (16,27)-(23,43): the door runs flush into the right wall; its right frame `k` at x=23 IS the wall outline, so there is no plaster to the right of the door and it sits in the building corner. Approved house-cottage centres the door with plaster each side. Fix: widen the hut and put the door about 3 px in from the right wall, or centre it and move the window beside it, with `C` between door frame and wall `k`. Door ramp is `w W W W W w` (dark on the left, against top-left light): make the left column `V` and the right `w`, matching house-cottage (`kVWWWWWWwk`).
3. [MAJOR] stalks (28,0)-(73,43): the silhouette reads as three spiky cacti or thorny beanstalks, not rows of crops. Leaves are 3x2 triangular spikes pointing out like thorns, the stem is a smooth 4 px pole with no nodes, and there are only three thin plants, not "rows". Fix: draw cane/corn-like stalks with a visible node every ~6 px (one `G` pixel across the stem), long drooping blade leaves (4-5 px, curving down and out, 1 px thick with `H` top edge) alternating left and right, and add a fourth stalk or a second, shorter row in front so it reads as a planted field. Keep only `H h G g` for foliage.
4. [MINOR] stalks: stem is `H h h G` between two `k` columns, i.e. parallel stripes following the outline (banding). Fix: 3 columns `H h G` or `H h h` with one `G` edge only on the right.
5. [MINOR] hut roof (0,10)-(25,22): lighting is only a top `e` row and a bottom `r` row; house-cottage has `e` along the left edge of each step and `r` down the right edge. Fix: add `e` on the left slope pixels and `r` on the right slope pixels.
