# Review round 1: batch fort

Set notes: all 7 sprites are the exact spec size; palette is clean (k u T t K stone, V v W w M wood/iron, B only in the flagpole). Outline, top-left light and crown height (6 rows, k row 5 across tower, gate, wall-front, wall-back) are consistent. Brick courses line up from the bottom across tower, wall-front and gate. The gate is 1 px shorter than the wall (spec-fixed), so its crown sits 1 px lower when bottom-aligned; not an art fault.

## fort: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] fort-crates (0,6)-(15,7): double-thick outline between touching crates. Row 6 (top crate base, x4-11) and row 7 (lower crates' tops, x0-15) are both `k`, and x7-8 is `kk` in rows 8-13, so every shared edge is 2 px thick against the 1 px outline used everywhere else. Fix: delete row 6 of the top crate and make row 7 the shared line, shifting the top crate down 1 px and adding its top row back so the sprite stays 16x14, or reduce the three crates to 7 px bodies sharing one `k` line, with the x7 column in rows 8-13 a single `k`.
2. [MAJOR] fort-crates (0,0)-(15,13): no top face. STYLE says land props are seen from the front and slightly above, but all three crates are flat front panels with no visible lid, so they read as flat tiles, and the chest and barrel in the approved set both show a top. Fix: give the top crate a 2 row lid at rows 1-2 in `F` (top row) and `V` (second row) with `k` outline above, then shorten its front to 4 rows. Do the same on the two lower crates' exposed top edges (the lid strip at x0-6 and x9-15, rows 7-8).
3. [MINOR] fort-crates (1,9)-(5,12): the seam row 3 is all `w` and reads as a stripe with the `V` top row, giving banding. Fix: keep the `w` seam only as 1 px between two `W` planks and drop the extra `w` at row 5 so the base is `k` directly under `W`.
4. [MINOR] fort-tower (8,0),(9,0)-(18,4): crenels are fully transparent down to the k row 5, so sky shows where the roof floor or rear merlons should be. Fix: fill x8-9 and x18-19, rows 3-4, with `K` (courtyard shadow) and leave rows 0-2 clear.
5. [MINOR] fort-tower (13,15)-(14,22): the arrow slit is solid `k` (same as outline) with a `K` cap that is lighter than the slit, so it reads as a black gash. Fix: make row 15 `kk`, and rows 16-21 `K` at x13 and `k` at x14, ending row 22 `kk`; slit stays 2x8.
6. [MINOR] fort-tower, fort-gate, fort-wall-front: every course is `u` / `T` / `T` / `t` full-width, so the walls read as horizontal stripes more than bricks (mild banding). Fix: break the `u` highlight row at the vertical joint columns (set the joint pixel in the `u` row to `T`) in the course rows of the tower and gate, for example (7,6),(15,6),(23,6) and the matching joint x on each later `u` row.
7. [MINOR] fort-wall-front (0,6)-(7,10): top course is 5 rows (`u`, three `T`, `t`) against 4 elsewhere, so the top course looks taller than the rest. Fix: delete row 8 (a `T` row) and add a `T` row to the base course so the height stays 24 and joint rows 12-14 are unchanged, or accept as noted.
8. [MINOR] fort-gate (13,9),(14,9): row 9 door has no centre seam (`ww`) while rows 10-21 have `kk`, so the seam starts 1 px low. Fix: set (13,9) and (14,9) to `k`.
9. [MINOR] fort-gate (15,9)-(18,21): right leaf is lit on its outer edge (`WWv` at right) mirroring the left leaf, but light comes from the top left. Fix: on the right leaf swap `W` to `w` in the two columns beside the outer `v`, e.g. rows 10-21 x16-17 `ww`, keeping the left leaf as is.
10. [MINOR] fort-wall-side (5,0)-(5,3): the vertical joint `t` runs 4 rows then stops with no horizontal mortar under it (row 3 to 4), so it tiles into dashes that look like cracks. Fix: add a mortar row `t` at row 3 across x3-8 (making two stacked courses of 3 and 4 rows), or remove the x5 joint entirely.

## Summary
fort: FAIL (0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR, 8 MINOR). The wall, tower, gate, back wall and flagpole are close to shippable; the crates need the outline and top-face fix before the family passes.
