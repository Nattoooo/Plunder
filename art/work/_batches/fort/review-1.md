## fort: FAIL
1. [MAJOR] fort-tower (0,7)-(13,17): the stone courses do not line up with the wall pieces it sits beside. Bottom-aligned (tower row = wall row + 6) the wall's mortar rows fall on tower rows 13 and 9, but the tower's mortar rows are 7, 11 and 15, so the course lines jump by 2 px at every wall/tower join. The bottom course is also a stub: rows 15 (t) and 16 (T) leave a single T row with no u lip above the bottom outline, unlike the full u/T/T course at the foot of wall-front and gate. Fix: re-cut the body (rows 4-16) bottom-aligned to the wall rhythm, each course u lip, T, T then t mortar: rows 14 u, 15 T, 16 T (bottom course, full); row 13 t; rows 10 u, 11 T, 12 T; row 9 t; rows 6 u, 7 T, 8 T; row 5 t; row 4 T (top partial course under the kkkk line on row 3). Mortar rows 13 and 9 then coincide with the wall's mortar rows. Keep the 2x4 `kk` slit centred on cols 6-7, moved to rows 6-9 (crosses the row 5 mortar? no, it starts on row 6) so it sits inside the u/T/T course and the following mortar row.
2. [MINOR] fort-tower (0,0)-(13,2): merlons are 4 px wide (`kuuk`) while wall-front, wall-back and gate use 3 px merlons (`kuk`). Same height and same crenel width, so it reads as a bigger corner tower and is acceptable, but the spec says battlements must match. Fix only if the artist wants strict matching: 3 px merlons would need a 15 px width, so leave as is.
3. [MINOR] fort-tower (4,4),(9,4),(3,8),(10,8): joints give 4-5 px bricks while the walls use 4 px bricks with joints every 4 px (wall-front cols 3 and 1). Fix: when re-cutting courses (issue 1), put joints at 4 px spacing (cols 3, 7, 11 on course A; cols 1, 5, 9, 13-adjacent on course B) so brick size matches the wall.
4. [MINOR] fort-gate (0,0),(13,0): the gate starts and ends with a crenel, so beside a wall tile whose last column is a crenel the left join makes a 2 px gap, the right join a 1 px gap. The centre merlon is 4 px wide (cols 5-8) against 3 px elsewhere. A symmetric 14 px gate cannot avoid one of these with a period 4 tile, so accepted, but the assembler must know the left join is 2 px. Fix (optional): none in the sprite; note it in notes.md.
5. [MINOR] fort-gate (3,5)-(10,10): door opening is 7 px tall including the arch (wood 6 px, rows 5-10) against the brief's "about 8 px" for a captain (9 px). It cannot grow without losing the battlement row, so accepted. Fix: none; do not shrink further.
6. [MINOR] fort-wall-back (0,3)-(3,3): joint pixel missing in the u lip row (`uuuu`), while wall-front has `uuut` in the same row. Fix: row 3 to `uuut` so the joint runs through the whole course like the front wall.
7. [MINOR] fort-wall-back (0,1)-(2,1): merlon is only 2 rows (`kkk`, `kTk`) against 3 rows on the front wall. Reads fine as the inside face but differs from "same merlons". Fix: none required.
8. [MINOR] fort-wall-side (1,3)-(4,3): the cross joint row `kTttTk` reads as a loose grey smudge on a 6 px strip. Fix: make row 3 `kTTTtk`-style? Better `kuTTtk` repeated with only a single t mortar pixel pair `kttttk` on row 3 so the joint spans the floor cleanly.

[MINOR] fort-crates (0,4)-(7,5): the two lower crates have different widths (4 px and 5 px outer) and highlight patterns (`VW` vs `VVW`), and no crate has a slat or brace, so they read as plain brown blocks.
