# Ship-parts — current review round 1

Independent review by icons_artist, who drew none of these sources. Read STYLE.md, BATCH-REVIEW.md, CRITIC.md, the brief, relevant specs.json entries and ship notes, all thirteen current source grids and family notes, and the latest approved reference. Opened the entire sheet-1.png and checked every enlarged grid and both 1x/3x contexts. All ten criteria checked for each family. No earlier current-session batch or family critiques exist. Every canvas is exact, palette legal and transparency hard.

As a set these are restrained overhead deck-cell objects with consistent closed k contours, upper-left light, named physical details and at most three face tones per material. Dark K openings are cavities, not additional surface shading. Tiny symbolic details are justified by the fixed five-pixel cell; no larger land-scale detailing is imposed.

## helm: PASS

A rounded wooden wheel surrounds a single brass hub. The wood ring and four radial contact directions read at deck scale; V/v/W is three tones and the k perimeter closes cleanly. Exact 5x5.

## rudder: PASS

The straight wooden blade uses V/v/w from left light to right shadow. Its top five rows occupy the stern cell and the remaining two extend aft as required. Exact 5x7, overhead, closed and centred.

## mast: PASS

A compact wood base is surrounded by four tan rigging attachment ends. Those deliberate corner points explain the rope pixels without texture noise; all outer edges are enclosed. V/v wood, f cord and exact 5x5 fit the deck-cell view.

## oar-bench: PASS

The broad wood seating face, lit upper-left edge and dark near edge read as a bench across one deck cell. V/v/w stays within three tones. Exact 5x5 with clean straight edges and no orphan decorations.

## crew-quarters: PASS

A dark opening lies between two wooden ladder rails, joined by one actual rung. The overhead ladder descends into the hatch and remains distinct from the closed cargo hatch. Exact 5x5, three wood tones and a closed contour.

## cargo-hold: PASS

Two iron straps cross the closed wooden hatch. Wood V/v/w and metal n/M/m each stay within three tones, with left/top light and lower/right shadow. Exact 5x5 and clear on both deck and water.

## galley: PASS

The compact iron stove has an attached lit chimney cap and visible orange fire through its central opening. N/M/m stays within three iron tones; the small centred glow is a physical firebox, not arbitrary surface highlights. Exact 5x5, overhead and outlined.

## powder-magazine: PASS

Four distinct wood caps occupy a regular two-by-two arrangement inside a closed outer contour and dividing cross. At fixed 5x5 each keg top is necessarily a one-pixel object; the quartet is intentional rather than texture. V/v/w establishes shared upper-left lighting.

## workshop: PASS

A three-pixel steel hammer head and a continuous wood handle rest on the wood bench. N/M/m lights the metal from the left; V/v/W keeps the wood within three tones. Exact 5x5 with no decorative scatter or redundant interior outlines.

## sick-bay: PASS

A centred red cross lies on a cot with light upper-left and shaded right/lower cloth corners. The cross is connected, the cot outline complete and the 5x5 top view legible at deck scale.

## crows-nest: PASS

The clipped-corner round basket has a lit upper-left wood rim, shaded lower-right rim and a real dark interior. V/v/w is three tones; K is the opening. Exact 6x6, symmetric geometry apart from lighting, no ground or drop shadow.

## ram: PASS

A clean centred point broadens through even contour steps into the iron plate. N/M/m shades left to right; the lower 5x5 occupies its cell while three rows project toward the bow. Exact 5x8, closed and clearly upward-facing.

## figurehead: PASS

A narrow forward head, broad shoulders/chest and narrow supporting stem identify a small carved figure rather than a featureless bar. y/O/o is three gold tones with top-left light. Exact 5x8, overhead and centred, with the head projecting beyond its deck cell.
