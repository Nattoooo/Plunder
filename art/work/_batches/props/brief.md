# Batch `props`

11 families. Draw every sprite listed below.

## Folder `props` rules

- Folder props, 15 sprites, all flexible. Crates, barrels and the bottle also float at sea as loot, so they should read on blue water as well as on sand. The wreck is seen from straight above.

## Families

### `chests` (new: draw from scratch)

- `chest` 14 x 11 (flexible size): Closed wooden treasure chest with gold trim
- `locked-chest` 14 x 11 (flexible size): Iron-banded chest with a padlock
- `gold-chest` 14 x 10 (flexible size): Open chest overflowing with gold coins

### `crate` (new: draw from scratch)

- `crate` 11 x 11 (flexible size): Wooden crate
- `lost-cargo` 12 x 14 (flexible size): Crate with a small red flag, marking cargo lost when your ship sank

### `barrel` (new: draw from scratch)

- `barrel` 10 x 11 (flexible size): Wooden barrel

### `bottle` (new: draw from scratch)

- `bottle` 7 x 10 (flexible size): Message in a bottle: green glass, cork, rolled paper inside

### `wreck` (new: draw from scratch)

- `wreck` 34 x 17 (flexible size): Broken, half-sunk ship hull with a snapped mast, seen from above

### `dig-spot` (new: draw from scratch)

- `dig-spot` 12 x 7 (flexible size): Small mound of disturbed sand where treasure is buried

### `x-mark` (new: draw from scratch)

### `claim-stone` (new: draw from scratch)
