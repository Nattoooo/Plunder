## sail-patched: PASS

The full sail has a clean domed belly pushed toward the bow, one physically located 2x2 repair patch and a straight dark wooden yard. p/C/c is restricted to cloth. The half and furled states roll the cloth onto an identical nine-pixel yard and hide the patch naturally. Exact 9x5, 9x3 and 9x2 canvases; no decorative stitches or stray pixels.

## oar: PASS

A two-pixel grip on the left, narrow connecting shaft and broad three-pixel blade on the right form one readable overhead oar. V/W/w stays within three wood tones, top surfaces are brighter than their lower edges, and the object remains visible on water and deck. Exact 7x2.

## flags: PASS

The fixed dark pole and tapered flying cloth identify each pennant at 4x2. Pennants use only R in the swappable cloth; the pirate variants use black K/k. Each animation changes only the free outer edge while the pole and attached cloth remain fixed. No invented skull detail is required by the plain-black-flag spec.

## fire: PASS

The two exact 3x3 flames have one connected bright core, orange body and stable base. Only the upper tongue moves between frames, making a clear anchored flicker. No outline, ground shadow or unrelated sparks.

## deck-people: PASS

The 3x3 crew has a named head, R/r shirt and separated navy legs; the captain has a black tricorn ahead of a red coat. Both are overhead, restrained and legible at deck scale. Shirt swap colours appear only on clothing, and no extra face pixels fight the tiny silhouettes.
