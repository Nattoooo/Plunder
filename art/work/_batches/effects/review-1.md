# Review round 1: batch effects

Set-level: all four families are outline-free, use only allowed palette characters (gold y/Y, white P, blues l/L, steel N/n), match spec sizes exactly (3x3, 3x3, 1x1, 2x1, 2x2, 2x1, 3x3, 5x5) and stay flat and minimal like the approved set. Light is top-left on the only lit sprite (cutlass-swing). Palette use is consistent and no swap colours are misused.

## sparkle: PASS
1. [MINOR] sparkle-2 (1,1): collapses to a single Y pixel, so it reads as a dot rather than a smaller star. Acceptable because a 3x3 box cannot shorten the arms; no change required.

## glint: PASS
1. [MINOR] glint-3 (0,0)-(1,1): solid 2x2 P block rather than the briefed plus; a plus cannot fit in 2x2, so the notes' deviation is justified.
2. [MINOR] glint-4 (0,0): `l` mid blue is close to the water colour and nearly vanishes on the sea; the sequence still reads as a fade. Optional: swap to `L` `l` order is not needed, ship as is.

## muzzle-flash: PASS
1. [MINOR] muzzle-flash (0,0),(2,0),(0,2),(2,2): the four y corners touch the Y centre only diagonally, so at 1x it reads as an X of dots. It is distinct from sparkle-1 (plus) and reads as a burst; ship as is.

## cutlass-swing: PASS
1. [MINOR] cutlass-swing (0,4)-(4,0): the blade is a straight 2 px stripe with a blunt tail (N at (0,4), n at (1,4)); a cutlass is curved. At 5x5 a curve would cost clean 1-1-1 steps, so keep it.
