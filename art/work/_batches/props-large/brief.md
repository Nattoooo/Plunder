# Batch `props-large`

3 families. Draw every sprite listed below.

## Folder `props` rules

- Folder props, 15 sprites, all flexible. Crates, barrels and the bottle also float at sea as loot, so they should read on blue water as well as on sand. The wreck is seen from straight above.

## Families

### `wreck` (new: draw from scratch)

- `wreck` 17 x 9 (flexible size): Broken, half-sunk ship hull with a snapped mast, seen from above

### `claim-stone` (new: draw from scratch)

- `claim-stone` 9 x 10 (flexible size): Carved marker stone with a white flag: an island you can buy
- `claim-stone-owned` 9 x 10 (flexible size): The same stone with a black flag: an island you own

### `shrine` (new: draw from scratch)

- `shrine` 17 x 12 (flexible size): Stone altar between two pillars holding a glowing green gem
