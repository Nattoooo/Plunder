## light-cannon: PASS

The narrow muzzle swells into a wider breech over a wooden carriage and two dark wheels. N/n/M gives left light and right shadow, and the closed outer contour fits the exact 5x7 canvas. The gun reads on both water and deck with no stray pixels or excessive detail.

## long-gun: PASS

Four aligned lit barrel pixels above a wide breech and short wooden carriage make the gun visibly longer and slimmer than the light cannon. The exact 5x8 canvas preserves a five-pixel deck-cell footprint; its barrel projects toward the bow. Closed contour and clean straight lines.

## carronade: PASS

The short broad iron barrel and compact slide distinguish it from the long guns. Three iron face tones describe the barrel; the dark lower seam is the occluded barrel/slide joint. Closed k silhouette, overhead view and coherent top-left light.

## swivel-gun: PASS

The thin barrel, small pivot yoke and wooden post form a readable overhead swivel gun. The pivot's centre is a real narrow metal spine; the V/v/W post now lights left and shades right. Exact 5x7 with a clean closed contour and no arbitrary points.

## mortar: PASS

The rounded 5x5 outline encloses a wide dark K bore and a lit upper-left iron rim. Its compact hollow form reads as a squat mortar at 1x. The shadow side merges into the dark outer edge at this necessary scale; no misleading extra detail is introduced.
