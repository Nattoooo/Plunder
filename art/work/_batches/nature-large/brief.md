# Batch `nature-large`

6 families. Draw every sprite listed below.

## Folder `characters` rules

- Folder characters, 22 sprites, all flexible. People and land creatures are seen from the front and slightly above, standing on the bottom row. Sea creatures are seen from straight above and face right, because the game rotates them to their heading.

## Folder `nature` rules

- Folder nature, 18 sprites. All flexible except the lava tile. Draw leaves with the four foliage colours from the table above, so each sea can recolour them (green in the tropics, grey-green in the north, brown in the volcanic south). Trees stand with the base of the trunk at the bottom centre; they are about twice the captain's height.

## Families
