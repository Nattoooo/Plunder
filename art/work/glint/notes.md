# glint

- glint-1: one white pixel (P), the first frame of the glint.
- glint-2: a three-pixel white line, the glint opening up.
- glint-3: a white plus at its brightest. The corners are open so it reads as a star point, not a 3 x 3 block.
- glint-4: two pale blue pixels (L) with a gap. The fade uses the palette's pale blue, so it drops clearly below white and still shows on the water.
- No outline: it is an effect, and a border would turn a glint into a box.
