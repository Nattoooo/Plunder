# ghost-lantern

Iron lantern frame with a teal ghost flame.

## Batch round 1
Left unchanged shape except the handle (6 px arch over the cap) and the flame, now a teardrop in I with i edge on plain K glass.

## Batch round 2
- 6 px handle arch, 12 px cap, glass 10 px at top widening to 12 px, 2 row base lip. Flame is a teardrop: 1 px tip, widening to 4, i on the right edge only. N glint on the left glass replaces the banded N/M columns.
