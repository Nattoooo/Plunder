# stern-lantern

## stern-lantern (3 x 3, fixed)

- **Brass ring (8 px):** a square brass lantern seen from above. Light comes from top-left, so the top and left edges take the highlight `y`, the top-right and bottom-left corners take the base `O`, and the bottom-right is the darkest `o`. Three brass tones is the most the material allows.
- **Centre (`Y`, pale glass):** the lit glass, the one bright pixel. It is the lamp's light and the reason the eye finds it on the stern.
- **No outline:** at 3 px on a side an outline would eat the whole lantern (STYLE.md allows this for very small sprites), so the brass ring is the silhouette.
- Fixed 3 x 3, as specified.
