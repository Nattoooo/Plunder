# house-shutters (20x21, flexible)
Same construction as house-cottage (approved), minus the chimney, so 3 rows shorter than the cottage's 24 rows; 21 matches spec.
- Roof: hip roof in swap colours e R r, light on top-left (e edge), shadow r on right and eave row. Outline k all round, no chimney so the top is a flat ridge.
- Wall: plaster C with c eave shadow under the roof, k base row on the bottom.
- Windows: two, each a 2 px glass pane (l) flanked by 1 px blue shutters (B), under a k lintel and over a k sill line. B is not a swap colour.
- Door: centred wood door (w dark top, W face) with a brass knob (y) at the right-of-centre, as in the cottage; 4 px wide, fits the captain.
Size: 20x21 as specified, no change.
