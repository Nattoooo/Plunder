# reeds (7x7, flexible, kept at 7x7)

- Two cattails. Left head x1-2 rows 0-2, right head x4-5 rows 1-3 (staggered down by one row). Each head is a 2 px sausage: v (lit left column), J (body), j (dark underside).
- Stalks: H under the left head at x1, G under the right head at x4. Transparent columns x2-3 separate them, so each reads as its own stem.
- Blades: two clumps at the base, left (h, H, G) lit, right (G, g) in shade, with a transparent column at x3 between them. Only the foliage swap characters H h G g are used on green parts, so each sea can recolour them.
- No outline, as with grass-tuft and flowers: a 1 px outline would eat the stalks. The brown heads carry the silhouette against the green.
- Bottom row touches the ground; the group is centred across the 7 px width.

## Batch round 1
1. [BLOCKER] Silhouette: the heads are no longer fused. They are 2 px wide and separated by transparent space above the blade row, so they read as cattails, not a pine or arrowhead. Three heads do not fit in 7 px with a gap between stalks, so the design uses two.
2. [MAJOR] Stub heads: each head is a 2 px by 3 px sausage, the same height as the other and staggered by one row.
3. [MAJOR] Stalks and outline: stalks show for two rows before the blades. The outline choice is none, as stated above, which matches grass-tuft. Heads are not outlined either, because a k cap would double the head width and close the gap.
4. [MINOR] Orphan corners: the blade corners are G/g now; no lone h pixels at the extremes.
5. [MINOR] Highlight: H sits on the left stalk and left blades; the right side uses G and g.
