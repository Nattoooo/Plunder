# sparkle

- sparkle-1 (large frame): a 3 x 3 body with a pale centre (Y) and gold arms (y), with one gold point on each side. The body corners are left open, so the outline is concave and reads as a star, not a gem or a cross. The centre is the only pale pixel, which gives the star its glow.
- sparkle-2 (small frame): the star shrunk to a 3 x 3 plus in the middle of the 5 x 5 box, with the same pale centre and gold points. It sits in the middle so the two frames swap without the marker jumping.
- No outline: it is an effect, and the outline would eat the 5 x 5 shape.
