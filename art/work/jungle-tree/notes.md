# jungle-tree (20x15, flexible, size unchanged)

- Canopy (rows 0-8): one wide flat umbrella, 20 px across and 8 rows deep, so it reads as a different tree from the round broadleaf. Outline is 1 px `k`. Light from the top left: `H` highlight cap on the upper left, `h` base, `G` shade on the lower right and along the underside. One small `G` clump at the middle breaks the flat area like the pilot tree. Only foliage chars `H h G` are used (no `g`), as the swap colours require.
- Underside (rows 7-8): `G` band narrowing to the trunk, closed with a `k` line, so the canopy reads as a roof over the trunk seen slightly from above.
- Trunk (rows 9-14): tall and thin, 2 px of wood (`v` lit left, `w` shaded right) between `k` outlines, centred. A one-pixel flare on each side at the base (row 13) and a `k` base row; touches the bottom row, no ground or shadow.
- Silhouette is symmetric apart from the shading.
