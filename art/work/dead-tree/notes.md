# dead-tree (nature) - artist notes

## dead-tree, 13 x 13 (spec 13 x 11, flexible; +2 rows)
Front view, slightly from above, trunk base on the bottom row at centre column 6. Two rows taller than spec so the trunk and forks read clearly next to the 9 px captain (the living tree is 18).

- Trunk (rows 4-11): 3 px wide wood, `v` lit left, `W` base, `w` shaded right, matching the living tree's trunk ramp. No foliage characters anywhere, so it is not recoloured.
- Root flare (rows 10-11): widens 1 px per side per row, like the living tree, then the bottom outline row.
- Limbs (rows 1-4): a left limb and a right limb fork off the top of the trunk and end in 1 px twig tips (outline only, as thin dead twigs), plus a short centre stub between them. The right limb is shaded `w` since it faces away from the light; the left is lit `v`. The fork is deliberately uneven (left leans out lower, right climbs higher) for a twisted, dead look.
- Bare silhouette with no leaves: forked branches read at once as a dead tree.

## Round 2 (redrawn; still 13 x 13)
Trunk now kinks: rows 5-7 sit one pixel left of rows 8-9, with the root flare below. The crown is 5 rows (0-4) above the kink and reaches higher than the trunk is wide.

1. MAJOR centre stub exposed: fixed by deleting the stub; the fork is now two limbs, and every limb tip has a `k` cap above it (rows 0).
2. MAJOR right limb tip open: fixed, `k` at (8,0) caps the `d` tip at (8,1).
3. MAJOR straight tube / Y-post: fixed. Trunk kink, left limb is a 1-1-1 diagonal with a secondary twig forking up at x4, right limb leans right and has a side stub at (8..9,3) capped by `k`.
4. MINOR banding: fixed. `v` fills the left two columns, `d` the right column, and a single `W` knot at (5,6) shows a branch scar; the flare has `W` as base tone.
5. MINOR colour: fixed. Right side uses `d` instead of `w`, which cools it against the living tree.
6. MINOR size: kept at 13 x 13; the taller crown justifies the two extra rows.
