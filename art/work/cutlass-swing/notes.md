# cutlass-swing

- Blade on the diagonal: each row moves exactly one pixel left, so the steps are a clean 1-1-1 and the edge stays straight.
- Light steel edge (N) on the top-left side, mid-steel body (n) in the middle, dark back (M) on the bottom-right. Light comes from the top left, so the bright edge faces it and the back falls in shadow.
- The back is two pixels wide where the blade is full, and one pixel near the tip, so the blade tapers to a point at the top right.
- Kept straight rather than curved: at 9 x 9 a curve adds steps that do not show, and the straight blade reads as a cutlass swing at game scale.
- No outline: it is an effect.
