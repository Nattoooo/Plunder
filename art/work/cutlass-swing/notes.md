# cutlass-swing

Steel blade of a cutlass pointing up and to the right, shown during a sword swing. Steel ramp only, no outline (effect). 5 x 5, fixed.

- The blade runs on the diagonal, two pixels wide, with one step per row and per column (1-1-1 steps) so the diagonal stays clean.
- Light steel (N) on the top-left edge: light from the top left catches the cutting edge.
- Mid steel (n) on the bottom-right side: the flat in shadow.
- The single N pixel at the top right is the point.
- No hilt or guard, since the brief asks for the blade alone.
