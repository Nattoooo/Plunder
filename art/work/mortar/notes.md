# mortar

## mortar (5 x 5, fixed)

- **Rim, row 1 (`N n M`), and the left edge at rows 2-3 (`N`, then `M`):** the iron lip of the mortar, lit from the top left. The highlight sits on the top-left of the rim and the shade on the top-right and lower left.
- **Mouth, rows 2-3, columns 2-3 (`K`):** the dark bore, wide, and most of the interior, because a mortar is squat and its mouth is its main shape.
- **Shadow side:** the mouth runs up to the outline on the right. Five pixels leave no room for a rim on both sides, so the outline stands in for the rim's shadow.
- **Round silhouette:** the corners of the 5 x 5 are transparent, so the mortar reads as round.
