# ancient-coin

Bronze coin with a worn cross mark and a thick edge.

## Batch round 1
Round 12 x 12 disc with y/o rim, 2 row lower edge for thickness, brass ramp o/O/y, off-centre K crown mark and a K wear nick lower right.

## Batch round 2
- Rounded disc: 3 step top, clean 2 row edge (o row then k row stepping in), no interior k pixels. One 4 px y arch mark above centre replaces the mouth and smudge.
