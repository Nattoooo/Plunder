# muzzle-flash

- White-hot centre (P), one pixel, so the eye lands on the shot.
- Pale yellow ring (Y) around the centre, which is the bright body of the burst.
- Gold spikes (y) out to the four sides and four corners. The corners are open between spikes, so the burst does not read as a square.
- No outline: it is an effect and sits over whatever was shot at.
