# bush notes
Both 10x6 (flexible, as specified), standing on the bottom row, symmetric outline, foliage swap chars only (H h G g; only h, H, G used), light top-left, shadow bottom-right.

## bush
Low round dome: rows widen from a 4 px crown to the full 10 px width, flat bottom outline since it sits on the ground. H highlight patch upper left, G shadow lower right. Reaches about knee height on the captain.

## berry-bush
Identical silhouette and shading. Three single R pixels are the berries, scattered on the lit and mid areas (not the shadow, where red would vanish), each surrounded by leaf so they read as fruit. R is not used for foliage, so the foliage swap is unaffected.
