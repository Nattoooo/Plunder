Slim barrel on an iron yoke (two arms) set on a wooden post.

## Batch round 1
Open yoke replaced by a solid pivot block with trunnion nubs; post uses v/w (V highlight at top); K bore on muzzle.

## Batch round 2
Barrel stouter (4 px interior) with the 10 px nub row in the middle, tapering to the pivot; post widened to 4 px interior (v V V w) and starts at row 9, centred.
