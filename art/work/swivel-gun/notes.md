# swivel-gun

## swivel-gun (5 x 7, fixed)

- **Muzzle, row 0:** the outline closes the top of the barrel.
- **Barrel, rows 1-3 (`k N k`):** a small 1 px iron barrel. Swivel guns were the smallest pieces on a ship, so the barrel is thin.
- **Yoke, row 4 (`M n M`):** the iron clamp that holds the barrel and turns it on its pivot. It is one row, so it reads as a joint rather than a block.
- **Post, row 5 (`W v W`):** the wooden stanchion the swivel sits on. In wood, because posts were, and it sets the iron yoke apart from the barrel.
- **Base, row 6:** the outline closes the post.
