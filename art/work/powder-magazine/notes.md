# powder-magazine

Four powder kegs, one per quadrant, split by a dark k cross. Each keg is a 2x2 block: V lit top-left, v on its top-right and bottom-left, w in the bottom-right shadow. Five pixels cannot fit four outlined kegs with separators, so the dark cross divides the kegs and they carry no outer outline. This is the one deliberate exception in the batch.
