# mushrooms (9x6, spec 5x3, flexible)

Size change: 5x3 cannot hold two readable capped mushrooms with a 1 px outline, so it is 9x6 (about half the captain's height at most, ground clutter).

- Tall mushroom (left): domed red cap, e highlight top left, R base, r shadow bottom right, one white P spot as the classic fly-agaric mark. Single cream stem (C) with outline, standing on the bottom row.
- Small mushroom (right): same colours, cap one step smaller, its stem one row shorter so it sits slightly forward/younger, bottoms aligned on the bottom row. Caps share an outline pixel column so the pair reads as one cluster.
- Only the ramps k, e R r, C, P are used; light from top left.
