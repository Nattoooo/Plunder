# flags (4x2, fixed: flag-1, flag-2, pirate-flag-1, pirate-flag-2)

- Pole: one column of `w` (dark wood) on the left, both rows, so the flag hangs from something real.
- Pennant (`R` only): one tone, because `R` is the game's pennant swap colour and any other tone would break the recolour. Its free edge tapers (3 px on top, 2 px below), so it reads as a streamer.
- Pirate flag (`K` base, `k` on the bottom edge): black with a shade on the lower edge, the same light rule as the rest of the set. The top-right notch alternates with the bottom-right, which gives the ripple.
- `-2` frames: the notch moves from the lower edge to the upper edge, which is the flag flapping. The silhouette stays the same size.
- No outline: 2 px high, the same call as grass-tuft and flowers.
