# silk

16x16 icon, 1 px k outline, light top left, shadow bottom right. Drawn from the approved 8x8 design with the same materials and ramps; extra pixels used for cleaner shape and form only. See art/src/silk.txt.

## Batch round 1
Redrew as two cylinders: rounded end cap with R ring and centre dot, E/e/R top-to-bottom shading, second bolt offset 2 px. Bolts share one k line; diagonal noise removed; E/e lead the colour, R only shadow.

## Batch round 2
1. Cap is now a real ellipse (5 px tall, 1 px stepped corners, E fill); the straight R column is gone and the body starts directly against the cap's right k outline. Body: E top, e middle, R bottom.
2. One clean spiral per cap: a 3 px R arc inside an E ring, nothing else.
3. Shared k row 7 now only holds the rounded corners of both bolts; no lone stubs.
4. Second bolt's spiral arc sits one row lower than the first, so the pair looks hand-stacked.
