# oar (7x2, fixed)

- Grip (left end): `w` in the top row and `WW` in the bottom row, so the grip is two pixels tall and thicker than the shaft, something for the hand to hold.
- Shaft (top row, `WWW`): one pixel of mid wood, running right from the grip.
- Blade (3x2 on the right): top row light wood `VVV`, bottom row mid wood `WWw` with a dark end. The blade avoids `v`, the deck colour, so it stays visible on deck.
- No outline: at 2 px high an outline would eat the whole oar, the same call as grass-tuft and flowers.
