# oar (7x2, fixed)

- Grip (`WW` at the left end of the bottom row, `w` at the very end): two pixels tall, thicker than the shaft, so the hand has something to hold.
- Shaft (top row, `wWWW`): one pixel of mid wood, with the dark `w` at the grip end.
- Blade (3x2 on the right): top row light wood `VVV`, bottom row mid wood `WWw` with a dark corner. The blade avoids `v`, which is the same colour as the deck, so the blade stays visible on deck.
- No outline: at 2 px high an outline would eat the whole oar, the same call as grass-tuft and flowers.
