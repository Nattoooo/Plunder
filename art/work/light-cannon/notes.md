Slim iron barrel, flared muzzle ring at top, swelling breech; wooden carriage with a wheel each side. Light from top left (N left, M right).

## Batch round 1
Barrel now runs down into the carriage as a breech (NM) between wood cheeks; wheels are 2 px wide and merge with the carriage; muzzle has a K bore; wood limited to v V w.

## Batch round 2
Barrel flare removed: straight 2 px barrel widening only toward the breech, K bore kept. Wheels (V/v left, v/w right) are one tone brighter than the all-w carriage, no k gaps, so they read as discs at the sides; breech NM only in rows 9-10.
