# mermaid-scale

Teal scale with ridge arcs and a blue tint on the right.

## Batch round 1
Teardrop scale point down, three stacked i arcs on I base, 3 px l crescent at lower right only.

## Batch round 2
- Wider fan scale (14 px) with a short 6-4-2 point instead of a stem. Two continuous i arcs plus a short inner arc follow the outline shape, 2 px apart, starting at the top and ending 1 px short of the outline.
