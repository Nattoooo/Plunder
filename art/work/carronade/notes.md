Short fat barrel with a dark breech band, on a stubby wooden slide with no wheels.

## Batch round 1
Muzzle has a K bore and the barrel tapers toward the mouth; breech band spans the full width; slide uses v V w only; the n wedge is a narrow stripe.

## Batch round 2
Barrel is now a straight 8 px cylinder with a K bore, 10 px trunnion band (rows 5-6) giving nub read, then a K breech band. Slide is a full-width 10 px base in v V w.
