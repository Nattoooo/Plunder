# carronade

## carronade (5 x 7, fixed)

- **Muzzle, row 0:** the outline closes the top of the barrel.
- **Barrel, rows 1-3 (`N n M`):** a 3 px fat barrel for the full width of the sprite. Short and wide in bore, which is what makes a carronade read as different from the thin light cannon and long gun. The highlight runs the full length of the barrel on the left, down to the breech ring, so the light stays consistent from top left.
- **Breech ring, row 4 (`K`):** a dark band where the barrel meets the slide, the shadow under the gun.
- **Slide, row 5 (`v v v`):** the wooden slide the carronade sits in. Carronades had no wheels, so there are none here.
- **Stern, row 6:** the outline closes the slide.
