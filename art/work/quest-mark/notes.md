# quest-mark (batch props-small)

- quest-mark 4x5 (spec 3x5, one px more): the stroke is 2 px wide (y lit left, O shaded right) because a 1 px gold stroke is lost against sand. The gap row is empty and the dot is 2 px (same two tones). No outline: a 1 px outline around a 2 px stroke and a dot needs more than the 5 px height, and effect-style marks such as the approved sparkle are drawn without one.
