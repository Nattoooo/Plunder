# golden-idol

Big grinning head with teeth on a small body, gold ramp.

## Batch round 1
Head cut to 5 interior rows with a k line under it; O shade on right column, nose and under brow; three 2 px P teeth; body of 6 rows with shoulders, belly mark and two stubby legs.

## Batch round 2
- Body narrowed to 10 px with shoulder notches, a short neck, a waist, and two 4 px legs with a centred 2 px gap (cols 7-8). Belly is a 6 px y band with Y/O arms.
