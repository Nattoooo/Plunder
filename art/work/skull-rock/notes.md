# skull-rock (15x10, flexible, size unchanged)
- Dome: stone U base with a u/T shadow band on the right and bottom-right (light from top left); no highlight tone needed.
- Eye sockets: two 3x2 k pits, symmetric about the centre column.
- Nose: 1 px k slit between the eyes, running down one row below them.
- Teeth: three blocks (2/3/2 px wide) split by k gaps, under a narrower jaw; sits on the bottom row, centred, outlined in k.
- Stone ramp only (no bone colours) so it reads as a pale rock rather than a real skull.
