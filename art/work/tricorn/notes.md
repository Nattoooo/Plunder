# tricorn

16x16 inventory icon, k outline, light from top left, materials on the STYLE.md ramps (see src/tricorn.txt).
The silhouette carries the identity; interior colour changes mark only real parts (handle, blade, guard, trim, glass).

## Batch round 1
Rebuilt from M/m with n highlight on the crown, two raised side corners and a downward front point, gold O trim on all brim edges.

## Batch round 2
- Body is m with M only top-left and K shadow on the lower right; crown is taller and joined to the raised corners with m fill.
- Gold bar replaced by a 1 px trim following the brim edge from (1,5) down to the front point (7-8,13); (13,5) is m.
