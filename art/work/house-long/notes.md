# house-long

30 x 23, as specced. Same construction as house-cottage: front view from slightly above, light from the top left.

- Roof (e R r swap colours only): hip roof, ridge 22 px wide, hips step 1 px every 2 rows like the cottage. Row 3 is the lit ridge cap (e), left hip e, right hip r, bottom course r above the k eave line. Eaves overhang the wall by 1 px each side.
- Chimney: stone u/T at the right of the ridge (x19-25), cap rows 0-1, off centre so it does not fight the centred door. Light side u, shaded right T.
- Walls (C c only): c top row under the eave, C plaster, k outline on sides and bottom.
- Windows: four identical 4x4 windows, k frame, l/B glass (light top-left, deep blue below), V wooden sill. Two each side, exactly mirrored about the centre.
- Door: 4 px wide, 8 px tall (the captain fits), w frame on top and left, W planks, one y knob on the right side of the door, standing on the bottom outline. Centred between x14 and x15.
