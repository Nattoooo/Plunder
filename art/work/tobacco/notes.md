# tobacco

8 x 8, fixed. Light from the top left; one-pixel k outer silhouette.

A bundle of dried leaves in the wood ramp. The silhouette flares at both ends and pinches at the waist, where the f string ties it. V and v are the lit upper leaves; W is the shadow side and the lower leaves. Redrawn: the earlier version was a plain block with a bright band, which read as a burger.
