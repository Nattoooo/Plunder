# jade-figurine

8 x 8, fixed. Light from top left.

Jade figurine, 8 x 8 fixed. Squat jade frog: two eye bumps at row 0, a dip at row 1, a body (H highlight, h, G shade) and two thin legs at the bottom corners with a foot line at row 7. Legs use k outlines because they are thin shapes at the silhouette edge.
