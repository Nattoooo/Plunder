# silver-coins

8 x 8, fixed. Light from top left.

Silver coins, 8 x 8 fixed. Stack of three coins seen from slightly above: the top face (N, rows 1-2) is the only lit top, each rim below (n) sits on a dark M seam (rows 4 and 6) so the coins read as separate. Steel ramp N n M, three tones. Outline k around the stack; no interior outline.
