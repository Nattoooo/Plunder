# brass (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Brass gear with four teeth and a dark hub hole.

- Brass ramp `y` (glint) / `O` (base) / `o` (shade), lit top left.
- Four teeth at the cardinal points; the top and bottom teeth are two px wide, the side teeth two px tall.
- Two by two `k` hub hole in the centre, the real hole of a gear.
