# tar (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Bucket of black pine tar with a brush standing in it.

- Tar surface `m`/`M`, inside the rim; brush handle `W` above the rim, bristles `J` in the tar, so the brush reads as dipped.
- Wood staves `V`/`v`/`W` with one iron hoop `M`, the same wood and iron as the barrel.
- Bucket tapers to the base, so it reads as a bucket and not a cabinet.
