# fire (3x3, fixed: fire-1, fire-2)

- Tip: a bright `Q` at the top centre in fire-1, the hottest and brightest pixel.
- Body (`qQq`): outer orange `q` with a bright `Q` core, so it reads as a flame and not a blob.
- Base (`qqq`): the full-width bottom row sits on the deck, as a fire does.
- fire-2: the tip moves to the top left; the core and base stay put, so it flickers without moving.
- No outline: effects take none (STYLE.md).
- Judgment call for the lead: this is a side-profile flame (tip up), not the straight-down view STYLE.md gives deck objects. A 3 px top-down fire would read as a glowing spot, and an orange spot risks reading as the yellow muzzle flash, so the flame was kept for readability.
