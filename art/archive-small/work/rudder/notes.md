# rudder

Five-by-seven blade. Rows 0-4 sit on the stern cell, rows 5-6 hang past it. A flat k top row is the attachment edge along the stern. Rows 1-4 are the blade: V lit on the left, v in the middle, w in the shadow on the right. The blade tapers to a point at the tail (`.kvk.`, then `..k..`). Two M pintles on the left edge at rows 1 and 3 are the iron hinges.

## Batch round 1

- MAJOR 1 (cylinder read, round caps): the top row is now a flat `kkkkk` attachment edge, and the tail tapers over rows 5-6. The old round caps are gone, so the silhouette reads as a flat blade, not a log. Rows 1-4 keep the `kVvwk` tones as the review's fix specifies.
- MINOR 2 (no hinge): added M pintles at (0,1) and (0,3), as suggested.
