# muzzle-flash

Yellow-white burst from a pistol or musket shot. Gold ramp only, no outline (effect). 3 x 3, fixed.

- Pale yellow centre (Y): the hottest part of the flash, the powder flare at the muzzle.
- Gold corners (y): sparks thrown diagonally out of the burst.
- The corners are left open rather than filled, so the burst does not read as a square block. A plus was avoided because the sparkle effect uses that shape.
