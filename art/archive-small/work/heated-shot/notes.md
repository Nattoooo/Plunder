# heated-shot (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

- Core `Q` orange with a single `y` glint at its upper left, the hottest point.
- Rim `e` lit to `R` shade, so the ball reads as hot from the outside in.
- Round eight px circle, the full square, the same outline as the rum icon.
