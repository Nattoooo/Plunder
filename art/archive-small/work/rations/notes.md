# rations (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Two stacked square hardtack biscuits, six px wide, light top left.

- Top face `F` (lightest tan) on the upper biscuit is the lit edge; front `f`, shade `D` on the right and bottom.
- Docking holes `D` on the top face at x2 and x5: the real holes of hardtack, placed inside the rim so they read as holes, not outline.
- A full `k` row between the biscuits is the only interior line: two shapes would otherwise merge into one block.
