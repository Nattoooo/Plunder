# Batch `effects`

4 families. Draw every sprite listed below.

## Folder `effects` rules

- Folder effects, 8 sprites, all fixed size. These are tiny, so keep them to a few bright pixels with a clear shape.

## Families

### `sparkle` (new: draw from scratch)

- `sparkle-1` 3 x 3 (fixed size): Gold four-point star marking dig spots and shrines, large frame
- `sparkle-2` 3 x 3 (fixed size): The same star, small frame

### `glint` (new: draw from scratch)

- `glint-1` 1 x 1 (fixed size): Sunlight glint on the water: a single white pixel
- `glint-2` 2 x 1 (fixed size): Glint growing: a short horizontal line
- `glint-3` 2 x 2 (fixed size): Glint at its brightest: a small plus shape
- `glint-4` 2 x 1 (fixed size): Glint fading: two pixels apart

### `muzzle-flash` (new: draw from scratch)

- `muzzle-flash` 3 x 3 (fixed size): Yellow-white burst from a pistol or musket shot

### `cutlass-swing` (new: draw from scratch)

- `cutlass-swing` 5 x 5 (fixed size): Cutlass blade pointing up and to the right, shown during a sword swing
