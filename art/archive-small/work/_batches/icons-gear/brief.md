# Batch `icons-gear`

18 families. Draw every sprite listed below.

## Folder `icons` rules

- Folder icons, 80 sprites, all fixed at 8 × 8 px. The inventory shows them at double size. One object per icon, centred and filling most of the square, at a slight angle from above. Keep the same outline and lighting across the whole set so the hold looks consistent.
