# Batch `ship-small`

5 families. Draw every sprite listed below.

## Folder `sails` rules

- Folder sails, 21 sprites, all fixed size, all seen from straight above. A sail's wooden yard (the pole it hangs from) is its bottom row, and the cloth bellies upward because the wind pushes it forward. Use only the sail cloth colours for the cloth, since every ship recolours its sails. Each sail comes full, half-reefed and furled (rolled up on its yard).

## Families

### `sail-patched` (has a drawing in art/src/sail-patched.txt)

- `sail-patched-full` 9 x 5 (fixed size): Small sail on the starter raft, full
- `sail-patched-half` 9 x 3 (fixed size): Small sail, half-reefed
- `sail-patched-furled` 9 x 2 (fixed size): Small sail, furled

### `oar` (has a drawing in art/src/oar.txt)

- `oar` 7 x 2 (fixed size): Wooden oar, handle on the left and blade on the right

### `flags` (has a drawing in art/src/flags.txt)

- `flag-1` 4 x 2 (fixed size): Pennant flag flying to the right, pole on the left (use the pennant colour)
- `flag-2` 4 x 2 (fixed size): The same pennant mid-ripple
- `pirate-flag-1` 4 x 2 (fixed size): Plain black pirate flag flying to the right
- `pirate-flag-2` 4 x 2 (fixed size): The same black flag mid-ripple

### `fire` (has a drawing in art/src/fire.txt)

- `fire-1` 3 x 3 (fixed size): Small flames burning on the deck
- `fire-2` 3 x 3 (fixed size): The same flames flickered

### `deck-people` (has a drawing in art/src/deck-people.txt)

- `crew` 3 x 3 (fixed size): A sailor seen from above, walking on the deck (shirt in the crew shirt colours)
- `captain-deck` 3 x 3 (fixed size): The captain seen from above at the helm: black tricorn, red coat
