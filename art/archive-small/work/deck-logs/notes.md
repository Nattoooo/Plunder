# deck-logs

## deck-logs (5 x 5, fixed)

- **Logs (cols 1-3, 3 px each, one per tile):** each log is a cylinder seen from above, running fore-aft (bow up), the same direction as the planks. Three wood tones show the round form under top-left light: `V` highlight on the left flank, `v` base, `W` shadow on the right flank.
- **Gap between logs (cols 4 and 0, `W W`):** the dark space between two round logs. It is 2 px wide because a rounded log's shadow flank and the gap next to it both fall in shade. Tiled, the logs and gaps repeat every 5 px.
- **Lashing (row 2, `D f f f D`, rope ramp):** one rope crosses every log and the gaps at the ends, tying the raft together. It sits at the same row in every tile, so the lashings form regular rungs across the raft. A 5 px tile cannot stagger them, and a single rope row keeps them calm behind parts.
- **Why not planks:** a 4 px flat plank with a 1 px seam (deck-planks) reads as boards. Logs need a round form, a wider dark gap, and a rope, so they read as timber.
- **No outline**, as STYLE.md requires for tiling tiles. Wood ramp only, three tones, no more.
- Wood characters `V v W` are also hull-wood swap characters, but deck-logs is not a swap part, so the game does not recolour it. Raft logs stay brown whatever hull they sit beside.
