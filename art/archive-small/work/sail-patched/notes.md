# sail-patched (9 px wide, fixed sizes 9x5, 9x3, 9x2)

- Yard (bottom row, all `W`): the spar the cloth hangs from. One mid wood tone so it stays visible on deck and on water. The approved sails use `v` in the middle, which is the deck colour and vanishes there.
- Cloth (`p` lit, `C` base, `c` shade): the only cloth colours, so every ship can recolour its sail. Light from the top left: `p` on the left three columns, `c` on the right edge, as in the approved sails.
- Belly: the top edge is a dome (5, 7, 9 px wide rows), so the wind pushes the cloth forward.
- Patch (full only): a 2x2 block of `c` just left of centre, the one detail that says "patched". In half and furled the cloth is rolled onto the yard, so the patch is out of sight.
- Half-reefed and furled: the cloth rolls up, so the bottom of the sail becomes one roll row above the yard.
- No outline: sail cloth is exempt (STYLE.md).
