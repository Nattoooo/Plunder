# iron-bands

## iron-bands (5 x 5, fixed)

- **Band (rows 1-2, full width):** a hoop of iron laid athwartships across the planking, so it runs across the fore-aft planks at right angles, the way a real iron band goes round a hull.
- **Top row `n` (mid steel), bottom row `M` (dark iron):** the top edge catches the top-left light and the bottom edge is in shade. Two tones are enough for a 2 px strip; a lighter `N` top would make the band look like a shine rather than metal.
- **Rows 0, 3 and 4 transparent:** the planks show through between bands, so the band reads as laid over the hull, not as part of it.
- **No rivets:** a rivet would be a single pixel repeated every 5 px across the hull. Bands alone say "iron" at this size.
- **No outline:** the band is an overlay on the planking, not a silhouette. An outline would turn a 2 px band into a 4 px dark bar.
- Full width, so neighbouring cells join into one continuous band.
