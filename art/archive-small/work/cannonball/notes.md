# cannonball (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Pyramid of three dark iron balls: two at the bottom, one on top.

- Each ball is four px with corners cut, lit `n` top left, `M` base, `m` shade lower right.
- The bottom balls sit either side of the centre column and the top ball sits over the gap, so the pyramid is symmetric.
- Iron ramp only, same as the other iron items.
