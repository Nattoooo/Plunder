# grog (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Wooden tankard of grog.

- Amber drink `y`/`O` shows across the top rim, so it reads as a drink and not as an empty bucket.
- Wood body `V`/`v`/`W`, iron hoop `M` at about two thirds down, same hoop as the water barrel.
- One-pixel handle on the right, `v` fill inside `k` outline: the mug is told apart from the barrel by its handle and its narrower body.
