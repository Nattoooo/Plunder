# water (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Small wooden water barrel, same proportions as the barrel prop (narrow top and base, bulge in the middle).

- Lit top band `V`, staves `v` with `W` on the right, matching the barrel prop's wood.
- Two iron hoops `M` across the widest rows, the iron ramp used by every hoop in the game.
- Top and base are four or six px wide with the bulge at eight px, so the silhouette reads as a barrel at 1x.
