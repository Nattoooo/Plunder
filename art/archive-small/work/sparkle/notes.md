# sparkle

Gold four-point star marking dig spots and shrines. Gold ramp only, no outline (effect). Both frames are 3 x 3, fixed.

- sparkle-1 (large frame): gold arms (y) around a pale centre (Y). The arms are the four points; the centre is the bright core of the glint.
- sparkle-2 (small frame): only the pale centre pixel. A 3 x 3 box cannot shorten the arms any further, so the twinkle collapses to its core. The two frames alternate for the twinkle.
