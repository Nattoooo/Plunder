# flags (4x2, fixed: flag-1, flag-2, pirate-flag-1, pirate-flag-2)

- Pole: column 0 of both rows, `w` (dark wood), so the flag hangs from something real.
- Pennant (`R` only): one tone, because `R` is the game's pennant swap colour. The free end tapers, three pixels on the top row and two on the bottom.
- Pirate flag (`K` with `k` on the lower edge): black with a shade on the lower edge, the same light rule as the rest of the set.
- Ripple (`-2` frames): the short end moves to the other row, so the flag flaps without changing size.
- No outline: 2 px tall, the same call as grass-tuft and flowers.
