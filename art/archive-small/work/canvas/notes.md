# canvas (icons, 8 x 8, fixed)

Rolled bolt of sail canvas, seen from the side.

- Cloth ramp `p` (lit) / `C` / `c` (shade), the sail-cloth ramp.
- Rounded ends (corner cut on the top and bottom rows) so it reads as a cylinder rather than a box.
- Six rows tall, centred vertically with one blank row above and below.
