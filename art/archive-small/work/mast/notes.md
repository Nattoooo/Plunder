# mast

Five-pixel mast base seen from above. The post is a round 3x3 disc inside a k outline, lit top-left: V on the top and left, v and w toward the bottom-right shadow. The rigging stays are two D pixels on the middle row, one at each side. They are the rope ramp's mid tone, darker than the lit post, so the post is the subject and the stays read as lines running out from it.

## Batch round 1

- MAJOR 1 (stays on the corners, inverted value): the post is now a rounded 3x3 disc. The stays moved off the corners to the sides of the middle row. The stays also changed from f to D. f is lighter than the lit post, so keeping it would leave the value hierarchy inverted. D is the rope ramp's mid tone, so the post is now the lightest subject. Known cost: the stays sit on the canvas edge, where a 5 px width leaves no room for a k cap, so the rope ends carry no outline.
