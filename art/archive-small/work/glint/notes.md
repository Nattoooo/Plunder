# glint

Sunlight on water. White (P) for the glint, fading to the pale blues (l, L) that sit on the water colour. No outline (effect). Sizes are as specified.

- glint-1 (1 x 1): one white pixel, the first point of the glint.
- glint-2 (2 x 1): two white pixels, the glint stretching sideways into a short horizontal line.
- glint-3 (2 x 2): a solid white block, the brightest frame. A plus needs five pixels and cannot fit in a 2 x 2 box, so the peak is a block.
- glint-4 (2 x 1): a mid blue and a pale blue. The brief says two pixels apart, but a 2 x 1 box cannot hold a gap, so the pair is separated by tone instead, which reads as the glint fading into the water.
